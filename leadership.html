04 · Leadership

Three generations,
one craft.

The House of Mani is a family house. Its leadership has passed from the founder's polishing wheel to his sons' factories to a third generation building the modern brands of the house — without ever letting go of the first principle: trust.

The Founder

Mathurbhai B. Sojitra

Founder & Chairman · House of Mani
"Every diamond carries a story, and my passion is to shape that story into something unforgettable."

He left Dhari at fourteen and arrived in Surat with nothing but determination. After four years of rigorous training he founded Mani Export in 1971 with a few polishing wheels — and built it into a house whose diamonds travel from Antwerp to New York. Six decades on, he still sees the same passion in his grandchildren that he had when he started.
