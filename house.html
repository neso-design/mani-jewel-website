01 · The House

Six decades of
true brilliance.

Every house begins with a single act of courage. Ours began when a boy of fourteen left his village for the diamond city — and never looked back.

The Origin
"Diamonds have always fascinated me. I would study their cuts, their clarity, and their beauty — and I knew that my future was in this craft."
Mathurbhai B. Sojitra · Founder & Chairman

An enduring passion for diamonds has shaped Mathurbhai Sojitra's journey since he left his village of Dhari, Gujarat, at the age of fourteen. Arriving in Surat — India's diamond capital — with nothing but determination, he immersed himself in the art of diamond cutting and polishing. What began with a few polishing wheels is today a house of over 2,000 artisans whose work travels the world.

Milestones

The journey of the House.

1966

A boy arrives in Surat

Fourteen-year-old Mathurbhai Sojitra leaves Dhari for the diamond city. Four years of rigorous training teach him to read the light inside a rough stone.

1971

Mani Export is founded

With just a few polishing wheels, Mathurbhai founds his own workshop — transforming a small operation into a business known for quality and precision, formally incorporated as Mani Export Pvt. Ltd.

1980s–90s

The world opens

From Antwerp to Bangkok, Hong Kong to New York — Mani diamonds begin travelling to the world's great trading centres, and his sons Anil, Alpesh and Chetan join the house, bringing a drive for innovation.

2001

Mani Jewel is born

Under Chetanbhai Sojitra, the house extends from stone to setting. Mani Jewel is established as a manufacturer and wholesaler of authentic, traditional and exclusive fine jewellery — express with better diamonds.

Mani World

The flagship rises

At the Gujarat Hira Bourse in Hazira, Surat, the house builds its flagship — a technology-led factory where diamonds and jewellery are imagined, cut, cast, set and finished under one roof.

2026

The Silver Jubilee

Twenty-five years of Mani Jewel. The third generation — led by Neet and Dhyey Sojitra, honoured in GJEPC's 40 Under 40 — turns legacy into scale, building the modern jewellery brands of the house.
