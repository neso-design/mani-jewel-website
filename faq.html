Partner FAQ

Before you ask.

The questions buyers actually email us — answered plainly. Anything missing, write to the house and we reply within the same working day.

Company & business

Do you sell directly to consumers?
No. Mani Jewel is a business-to-business manufacturer and wholesaler. We sell to jewellery retailers, chains and trade buyers — your customers stay yours.
Do you have a minimum order quantity (MOQ)?
There is no rigid MOQ. Wholesale programmes are typically built from a tray upwards, but the custom programme accepts single-piece commissions. Tell us what you need and we will size the programme to it.
Do I need a business licence or GST number to buy?
Yes — as a B2B house we onboard registered businesses. For Indian partners that means a GSTIN; overseas buyers provide their local business registration and import credentials.
Will you make our own-brand (private label) collection?
Yes. Private label is one of our eight services — design, manufacture, branding, packaging and campaign imagery, delivered retail-ready under your name. We work confidentially; we never disclose whose collections we make.

Products & quality

What certifications do your products carry?
Gold is BIS hallmarked; diamonds are certified, graded in-house with Sarin precision planning. The house holds ISO 9001 and is a member of the Responsible Jewellery Council. See our responsible sourcing policy.
Which metals and purities do you work in?
14kt and 18kt gold in yellow, white, rose and green, plus platinum and silver — across all ten families, from necklace sets to maang tikkas.
Can you match a design we already sell?
Bring a drawing or a photograph — our four-step customisation service rebuilds it in CAD to 0.01 mm, shares cost and timeline upfront, and casts once you approve the mould.
How do I see the full catalogue?
The complete style catalogue — hundreds of live styles with codes, metal weights and diamond weights — opens to partners on enquiry. A dated stock sheet of available pieces is also sent monthly on request from the partners page.

Pricing & payments

How is pricing quoted?
Against the day's metal rate plus making and stone value — itemised per style code, so you can compare piece to piece. Vertical integration means one margin, not four.
Do you offer credit terms?
Established partners are offered terms appropriate to their history with the house. New accounts begin on standard terms; your representative will walk you through the path.
Which payment methods do you accept?
Bank transfer (NEFT/RTGS) for Indian partners and standard trade instruments for export orders. Details are confirmed on your pro-forma invoice.

Shipping & lead times

Where do you deliver?
Everywhere in India — Kashmir to Kanyakumari — and to the world's trading capitals. Deliveries are insured and documented door to door.
What are typical lead times?
Catalogue styles ship against stock or the current production cycle; custom and private-label programmes carry a timeline agreed at step two of the customisation process, before you commit.
Can you handle export documentation?
Yes — the house has exported since the 1980s and handles trade compliance, customs documentation and insured freight as part of the service.

After-sales & support

Who is my point of contact after I order?
The same named representative who took your order — one dedicated desk per partner, for follow-ups, re-orders and anything in between.
Do you repair or restore pieces?
Yes — professional repair, polishing, rhodium plating and stone replacement for partner stock, handled at Mani World.
What if a piece arrives with an issue?
Tell your representative within 48 hours of receipt. Every piece is inspected before dispatch, and anything that slips through is made right — repaired, replaced or credited.
